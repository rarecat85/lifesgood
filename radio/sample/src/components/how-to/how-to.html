How to – make your song

Just a couple of taps — it’s that easy.

❶ Step into Radio Optimism.

Tap ‘Let's Make a Hit’ to join and begin your story. Choose your preferred language to get started.

❷ Tell your story.

Who are you, and who’s this track for? The more you share — a memory, a moment, or something only you two know — the better your lyrics will be. Once you’re done, we’ll turn it into a track and custom cover art.

❸ Pick your vibe.

Time to shape the sound. Choose a genre — from hip-hop to K-pop — and pick a vibe that matches your story.

❹ Your track is ready!

Send it to someone ㅡ and let it play on Radio Optimism.
