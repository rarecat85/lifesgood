Maximizing blue carbon to combat climate change

Blue carbon, carbon stored in marine and coastal ecosystems, is a key natural solution for climate change mitigation. We aim to contribute to combating climate change by restoring and protecting these ecosystems that sequester carbon up to five times faster than terrestrial forests.
