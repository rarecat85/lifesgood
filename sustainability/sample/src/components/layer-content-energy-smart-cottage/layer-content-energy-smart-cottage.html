Korea's first prefab smart housing with the highest ZEB certification

The LG Smart Cottage Mono represents a breakthrough in sustainable housing, achieving Zero Energy Building (ZEB) Plus rating through prefabricated construction methods. This all-electric smart home integrates LG Electronics' AI appliances, advanced HVAC technology, and a rooftop solar photovoltaic (PV) system to achieve energy self-sufficiency.

  • Zero Energy Building (ZEB) Certification: Plus Rating
  • Energy Self-sufficiency Rate: 101.06%
  • Annual Primary Energy Consumption per Unit Area: -233.3 kWh/m²yr

* Certification Date: May 22, 2025
