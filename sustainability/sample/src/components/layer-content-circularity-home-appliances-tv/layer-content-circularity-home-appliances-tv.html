Eco-friendly pulp mold cushioning

By introducing 100% recycled pulp molds, we ensure the safe packaging of large home appliances weighing over 20kg. We have reduced carbon emissions during distribution by optimizing packaging to improve loading efficiency, and we are leading resource circulation by replacing single-use Styrofoam (EPS) with reusable materials. From carbon reduction to a plastic-zero future, we are realizing sustainable packaging solutions.
