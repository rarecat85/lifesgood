Reducing microplastic emissions by up to 60%

By applying Swing and Tumble motions to minimize fabric friction, we reduce microplastic emissions by up to 60%. Based on Intertek test results from July 2023. This figure compares the Microplastic Care course and the Mixed Fabric course (Model: F4Y7EYPBW) using a 3kg load of 100% polyester training jackets. The results were obtained by measuring the amount of microplastics collected through a 20㎛ filter. Actual results may vary depending on the type of fabric and washing environment.

* Model: F4X7009TBB
