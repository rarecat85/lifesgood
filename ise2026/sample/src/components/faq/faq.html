  • At ISE 2025, LG showcased a diverse lineup of cutting-edge products, including:

    1. Meeting Room Displays: The UP5Q display, featuring fire safety certification and 4K OSD, was highlighted for its high-performance capabilities. A 105-inch Stretch Signage display demonstrated a 21:9 format designed for advanced video conferencing setups.
    2. LG CreateBoard Series: Multiple models of LG CreateBoard were displayed, offering hands-on experience with interactive screens in a smart classroom environment.
    3. LED Technology Zone: The LG MAGNIT Micro LED Screen was featured alongside a range of indoor and outdoor LED displays.
    4. Integrated Software Business Solutions: Each zone included tailored business software solutions like LG Business Cloud, allowing visitors to explore how LG products integrate seamlessly into real-world business environments.

    For more details, visit LG official ISE 2025 showcase page: LG ISE 2025.

  • At ISE 2025, LG captivated attendees with two major innovations:

    1. Kinetic LED Display : A 7,180 x 4,090 mm screen composed of 88 connected LED panels. Its rotating cube-shaped modules created dynamic visual effects enhanced by AI integration. Visitors could upload photos via QR code, which LG’s generative AI transformed into Pop Art-style images displayed on the Kinetic LED.
    2. AM Micro LED Display : The 136-inch AM Micro LED with a 0.78mm pixel pitch was a technological marvel, hailed by TechTarget as the most complete AM display product at ISE 2025.
